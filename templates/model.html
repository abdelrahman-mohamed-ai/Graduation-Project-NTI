What the model pays attention to

Global XGBoost importance · summed across encoded categories

Importance describes model usage, not causation or the direction of a particular student's risk. Local SHAP explanations appear in student analyses.

Useful insights. Responsible limits.

  • Risk is an estimate, not certainty.
  • The source represents one higher-education context.
  • Validate and retrain using the institution's historical data before real deployment.
  • Sensitive demographic inputs require ongoing fairness monitoring.
  • Semester-2 features are intentionally excluded to prevent leakage.
  • Recommendations are advisory. Academic advisors make the final decision.
