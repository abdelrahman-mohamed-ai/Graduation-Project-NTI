THREE OUTCOMES, DISTINCT MEANINGS

Enrolled does not mean Graduate.

The operational model learns from resolved outcomes: Graduate = 0 and Dropout = 1. Enrolled students have unresolved outcomes and form the current-student scoring cohort.
